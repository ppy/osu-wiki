About time we season up the game with some spectacular spooky goodness!

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-10-20-halloween-2026-fanart-contest-results/banner.jpg)

It's no doubt that Halloween and osu! go hand in hand, especially with **2,483** of you casting a total of **30k+** votes to decide which entries haunt the main menu.

The votes have been tallied, the spirits have spoken, and the results are in!

## Finalists

**Tip**
You can click on any of the banners to view the full image.

[![](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/banners/Dreamxiety.jpg)](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/winners/Dreamxiety%20%2813103233%29.jpg)

## peppy's picks

As per tradition, we can't have a fanart contest without [peppy](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/2) blessing the main menu with even more eerie treats.

[![](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/banners/Ch3rryL.jpg)](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/winners/Ch3rryL%20%2820272150%29.jpg)

[**Ch3rryL**](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/20272150)'s cutesy depiction of both pippi and Mocha accompanied by lil' Don stroke a chord on peppy's heart this Halloween.

[![](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/banners/zynnu.jpg)](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/winners/zynnu%20%2819421965%29.png)

[**zynnu**](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/19421965)'s pippi and Mocha are celebrating their podium finish in this Halloween's costume contest with one cutesy attire and one that would definitely send chills down any osu! player's spine.

[![](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/banners/yasyrus.jpg)](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/winners/yasyrus%20%2814856576%29.png)

[**yasyrus**](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/14856576)'s artwork depicting a swarm of Dons may be one of the scariest pieces of the season. Handling two drums is already difficult as it is, let alone an entire school of sentient percussion specialists...

[![](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/banners/-%20Y%20u%20m%20i%20J%20i-.jpg)](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/winners/%2D%20Y%20u%20m%20i%20J%20i%2D%20%287656048%29.jpg)

[**- Y u m i J i-**](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/7656048)'s work of a cozy temple reunion between Jiangshi pippi and Mocha was this season's best (and only!) Chinese representation suited for these festivities.

[![](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/banners/Raybean.jpg)](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/winners/Raybean%20%2816676388%29.png)

[**Raybean**](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/16676388)'s paper texture collage (with some design help from [Dreamxiety](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/13103233) or so we hear!) of pippi cosplaying as Remilia Scarlet was one of this season's most creative compositions! The mix of cut-outs and theming of their work made it stand out from the rest, marking it well worthy of inclusion.

[![](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/banners/XETTE.jpg)](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/winners/XETTE%20%2839304029%29.jpg)

[**XETTE**](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/39304029)'s got the whole squad lined up in a vivid, colourful yet simple composition. Packed full with details, everyone's costume manage to stand out on their own — yes, even Chirou's!

---

We express our eternal gratitude to everyone who participated, both to the new faces and returning artists alike!

You'll find a selection of the winning entries shown above on the main menu, so make sure you turn on seasonal backgrounds in the settings if you haven't already. Note that one or more entries were omitted for this round as being deemed not a great fit for in-game display.

If you don't see your favourite entry, feel free to [download a zip file containing all entries of the Halloween 2026 Fanart Contest here](https://assets.ppy.sh/contests/296/Halloween2026FanartSubmissions.zip)! Maybe it's about time your desktop wallpaper got a proper dress-up for Halloween.

If you have [osu!supporter](/wiki/osu!supporter), you can drag and drop any image into the game's main menu to set it as the background (by itself — it won't join the others in the rotation). Even if you don't have an osu!supporter tag active, the **top 4 entries** will be available as part of the selection of **default profile page banners** until the end of the next art contest (click the pen icon at the bottom right of your profile cover).

Of course, all vote counts and placements are also visible on the [contest page](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/contests/296).

Happy Halloween and see you next time!
