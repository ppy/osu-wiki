The summer sun is fading... but Project Loved never does!

![](/wiki/shared/news/banners/project-loved-2.jpg)

Welcome back to your regular monthly instalment of "let's see what maps these people want to give leaderboards to". If you have no idea what that means, let me explain: Project Loved hosts community voting polls each month to decide which beatmaps get added to the [Loved category](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets?s=loved). All of the maps are chosen by the [Loved captains](https://loved.sh/team) of their respective game modes. Each beatmap banner in this news post will redirect you to the respective poll. Alternatively you can view them all directly on the [Project Loved forum](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/forums/120). The voting process lasts for 10 days, so don't forget to vote! More info can be found on the [wiki page](/wiki/Community/Project_Loved) and [website](https://loved.sh/).

Before going into the picks, there's a news item to get to:

- Our friend [Skill](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/7802027) is retiring from his tenure as part of the osu! captains, after 4 years of picking maps for everyone to enjoy. Please thank him for his many contributions!

136.5 BPM, 2:53 | 7.26★\
*description written by [Vendelicious](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8818089)*

*"I Have Determined to Make This Life Resound"* —[syaron105](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8741695)?

No, he didn't actually say that, as far as we are aware. However, he certainly has made his presence resound in osu!taiko both as a player and a mapper! Former #1 player [syaron105](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8741695) was known for being so good that there was a need for new maps and even new types of maps to challenge him properly. He didn't push that boundary alone, of course, but he did his part in mapping difficult and new style maps. So much so that today, if you call a map a "syaron map" (despite it not actually being mapped by [syaron](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8741695)), there is a good chance any veteran osu!taiko player knows roughly what kind of map it is. This is to say "syaron map" can almost be considered a genre of mapping, which speaks to the influence of his maps.

[syaron105](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8741695)'s maps are most known for being difficult maps, very high star rating, and having advanced or unusual tech. This map is a little more reserved, though. That being said, [I Have Determined to Make This Life Resound](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/1914287#taiko/3949175) is still not easy, sitting above 7 stars while being endowed with this unconventional (but great) style. The map features a very tasteful combination of 1/3 and 1/4 snapping at double BPM that makes it very fun to play. This map seems to be aimed more at being fun rather than purely being difficult.

I encourage those who are brave enough to try playing syaron maps. If you are new to these types of maps but feel like you are up for it, this map is a great place to start. If you are already an avid player of these types of maps, I'm confident you will enjoy this one too~

Thank you, [syaron105](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8741695), for making your time in osu!taiko resound!\
Give the map a try if u dare, and vote a **resounding yes** if you are determined to see it in Loved \<3
