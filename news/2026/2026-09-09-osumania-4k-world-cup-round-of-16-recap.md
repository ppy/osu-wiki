The knockout begins! While pressure settles in on our players, the competition remains as fierce as ever.

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-09-09-osumania-4k-world-cup-round-of-16-recap/banner.jpg)

Round of 16 tends to be one of the most bittersweet moments of the entire tournament, bar Finals and beyond. The prospect of being eliminated from the bracket at the earliest stage possible is never a good feeling to have. Yet those who manage to pull through are but one step closer to succeed! The question remains whether players can keep up with the pool's ever-increasing difficulty.

Despite the hurdles, however, no team has been willing to give up. No team has been eliminated without putting up a fight, and most winning teams have had to put some effort in just to stay afloat. As such, when we're getting these many good matches so early on, we can only be left wondering how the later rounds will pan out.

And what better way to showcase this than by enjoying our weekly video summary, brought to you by [Dynascape](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8784587), [SunApple](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11817622), [Normiplier](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10069850), [akace100](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/9308128), and [Rush_FTK](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/3046856). No reason not to enjoy it, even if you boast of a short attention span.

To get into the pool itself, perhaps the best starting point is [The Flashbulb - The Bridgeport Run](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2608594#mania/5839959) (RC6) by ::{ flag=US }:: [-MysticEyes](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/6253266)! After taking a break from MWC in 2025, -MysticEyes returns once more with another great chart. As expected of a song by The Flashbulb, you're more than certain to get through bursts and tricky rice patterns. Things only get more interesting by the chorus as players got introduced to dump sections backed up by the vocals, allowing for a more difficult and faithful representation of the song.

Of course, MWC wouldn't be MWC without the fated HB3 slot, and [ALEPH - SIGNALBURNERRR](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2608610#mania/5839987) by ::{ flag=TW }:: [\[Crz\]FolAH1217](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/6232458) is one of the more unique additions this round. It's rare to see such a free-form approach on a breakcore track, but the fact that this works so well almost feels like black magic. There's a rather interesting pattern that appears twice in the chart, and instead of spoiling it here for those unaware, we'd suggest you look at the chart yourself. We promise you'll be sure to like it!

As for the obligatory Nanahira cameo of this year's tournament, we got [Camellia feat. Nanahira - Energy * Drin-ko Fein-chan! (Cut Ver.)](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2608611#mania/5839988) (LN1) all thanks to ::{ flag=AU }:: [fvrex](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11863699). The coordination approach taken for this song works shockingly well, along with the actual playability behind the chart. One could argue that the real challenge is, well... listening to the song. It's an acquired taste, to put it mildly. We're sure that MWC's strongest soldiers would never falter to something as small as that, though!

[Steamtekk](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2608577#mania/5839930) (RC4) charted by ::{ flag=BR }:: [AutotelicBrown](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/4238941) is a unique, steampunk-styled song by Kolaa, who took a very different approach in comparison to their other songs, which ended up working out nicely. For the second week in a row, we got to experience this week's tiebreaker, the ~~mis~~fortune of which fell on the shoulders of the Canadians and Singaporeans. While their match was already quite close all things considered, the somewhat commanding team lead of **19,467 points** for the Canadians on their very last map sealed the victory for what was arguably the closest win of the tournament (so far).

While Qatar found their departure from the tournament rather early, we want to extend our gratitude to the players for their efforts. Just being able to get a spot in the bracket stage, let alone compete shoulder to shoulder with the world's best players, is something they should all be proud of.

Lastly, while our top 5 seeded teams remain undefeated, the Russian Federation found themselves in a rough spot by losing their winner's bracket slot against the Australians. After a hard-fought match versus the Aussies, they have no choice but to win all matches to come if they wish to remain in the tournament.
