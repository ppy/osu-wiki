osu! is a rhythm game. **k_Hwa** creates music that suits rhythm games. We'll let you piece that together.

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/573/header.jpg)

Transforming the circumstances of a harsh reality into pure motivation, **k_Hwa** (also known as *\$k\_Hwa\$*) has been knee-deep in music production since 2023. Two years later, **k_Hwa** released their debut album *PROXIMITY*, including tracks originally created for a variety of tournaments across osu! (you may have heard these before!).

**k_Hwa**'s music draws influence from EDM artists like [*Zekk*](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/76) and [*rejection*](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/199), along with not-so-electronic bands like *twenty one pilots*, resulting in an iconic drum-forward soundscape that's now part of our Featured Artist catalogue.

More specifically, **11** tracks can be found on [**k_Hwa**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/573) as pre-timed beatmap templates. If you're not sure what to do with those, try dragging them into your game client. You can figure it out from there.

Clean out your ears. You'll want to hear this:

### $k_Hwa$ vs. nominomu - .hereafter

Created for [Kyoku 2024](/wiki/Contests/Kyoku/2024), [this map of *.hereafter*](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2242883) by [Pisapou](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/16640021), [Shiraya Sayuki](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/19077461), [nominomu](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/30485380) and [Iris_Syen2](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10444226) achieved 6th place in the contest. Created for osu!'s Ranked section, [this osu!taiko map of *.hereafter*](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2472263) by [hz404](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/14947043) is also pretty cool.
