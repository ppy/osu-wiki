Ever wanted to become an osu!catch mapper? Whether you are a mapper from a different mode or a catch player looking to get into mapping, this might be your chance! We're happy to announce the Orchard Cup, a new mapping contest that is exclusive to novice osu!catch mappers.

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-08-31-orchard-cup-2026/banner.jpg)

The Orchard Cup takes heavy inspiration from the renowned [Newspaper Cup](https://osu.ppy.sh/home/news/2026-01-14-newspaper-cup-2026), which is a mapping contest catered towards novice mappers. Usually deemed the smallest community within the mapping ecosystem, the goal is to bring people into osu!catch mapping and put these newer creators in the spotlight. Maybe you could be our next talent! 

Orchard Cup 2026, the 1st occurrence of this event, is hosted by ::{ flag=AT }:: [Daletto](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/7592136) and ::{ flag=US }:: [Ascendance](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/2931883). We welcome and strongly encourage novice osu!catch mappers to participate!

Please join [our official Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/XZXAhqjMx) for the latest updates.

## Rules

Before you start working on your submission, please make sure you familiarise yourself with the rules below.

- This contest is for the **osu!catch mode only**.
- You must submit through the [Orchard Cup 2026 page in the contest listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/contests/295).
- Your submission must be an `.osz` file exported from the osu! in-game editor. You must submit at the designated submission site.
- Your submission must use a beatmap template from the [Featured Artist library](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists). You are **not allowed to change the artist and title fields**.
- Your submission must comply with the [Ranking Criteria](/wiki/Ranking_criteria/osu!catch), except for rules and guidelines that cannot apply (e.g. spread requirements).
- Your submission must **not contain information that reveals your identity**.
- You must complete your submission independently. **Do not upload or share** your submission with others before the results' announcement.
- Beatmaps made by **generative artificial intelligence** are **not allowed**.
- You are allowed to use **custom hitsounds, backgrounds, and custom combo colors**. However, you are **not allowed to add videos or storyboards** unless they were already present in the Featured Artists' templates. Backgrounds must follow the platform's [**visual content considerations**](/wiki/Rules/Visual_content_considerations).
- **You are required to finish one and only one difficulty** in your submission.
- Drain time must be **at least 2 minutes** and **at most 10 minutes**.

Additionally, **OC 2026 is exclusive for novice mappers.** You must satisfy all the following eligibility criteria:

- You must have joined osu! **before the 1st of June, 2026**.
- You **do not** have the "Elite Mapper" title or are a winner of any other osu!catch mode mapping contests (e.g. Duo Dash or Catch Mapping World Cup). *(The cut-off date is June 1st, 2026.)*
- You have **never been a member** of mapping-related groups (NAT, QAT, BAT, MAT, BN, etc.) in the osu!catch mode.
- The total drain time of your ranked [Rain (and above) difficulties](/wiki/Beatmap/Difficulty) **before June 1st, 2026** must be **below 10 minutes**. Collab difficulties are counted by corresponding sections mapped.
- Throughout the contest, the hosts may inspect if you qualify for novice status.

## Songs

There will be **one category to compete in** for OC 2026:

- **Featured Artist**: You are free to choose any track listed in the [Featured Artist library](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists), including osu!originals. Cutting is **NOT** allowed.

Please use the templates provided from the listings above and make sure you do not change the artist and title metadata fields.

## Judging

| Criterion | Explanation |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Execution (50%)** | Execution refers to how well the map reflects the music elements, with emphasis on music reflection, pattern/rhythm consistency, coherence, and playability. As OC is for novice mappers, judges will focus more on **solid fundamental execution** as the primary objective. |
| **Creativity (35%)** | How the map stands out compared to current mapping trends and other submissions. Generic maps or copy-paste concepts are discouraged. Creativity built on top of solid expertise is highly valued. |
| **Judge's Impression (15%)** | Judges may award or deduct points based on noteworthy aspects, such as outstanding sections, hitsounds, or notable weaknesses. |

Judging scores will be **standardised** and submissions are **anonymous** to the judges.
