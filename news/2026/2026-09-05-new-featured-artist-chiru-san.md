The wait is over. **Chiru-san** is now part of our Featured Artist library!

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/564/header.jpg)

2022 marked when **Chiru-san** plunged into music production. Only a year later, he released his track *Moongazer*, establishing the anime-infused dubstep sound he's known for today.

With influences like *Porter Robinson*, *Madeon*, *Phritz* and *Demotapes*, it's no surprise that **Chiru-san**'s impactful soundscapes resonate with eardrums and emotions alike. That iconic sound is also why he's part of our Featured Artists as of today!

The situation is simple: **12** pre-timed `.osz` files have been added to [**Chiru-san**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/564), and your mission is to map one (or all) of them.

Before hopping into the editor, you might want to draw some inspiration from these maps:
