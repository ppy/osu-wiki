A revival worth putting under the Spotlight~~s~~.

![](/wiki/shared/news/banners/beatmap-spotlights-new.jpg)

For the longest time, the Beatmap Spotlights have served as a way to encourage players to try out newly added features to the osu!(lazer) client under a controlled competitive environment. While promoting cool and novel beatmaps was one of its objectives, it was never its main one. As such, map selection always had to be curated with accessibility and difficulty balancing constraints in mind, which limited the beatmaps we could spotlight at any given time.

This all changes today! As osu!(lazer) gains more traction on a daily basis, the value that Spotlights once brought to the new client has sharply diminished. While we will not be foregoing the concept of playlists, we have changed gears to take a more *mapping-centred* approach where maps and their respective mappers get the attention they rightfully deserve.

## What's new?

Over these past few months, a handful of [Beatmap Spotlight Curators](/wiki/People/Beatmap_Spotlight_Curators) (BSC) have been working with the [Nomination Assessment Team](/wiki/People/Nomination_Assessment_Team) (NAT) in an attempt of solving a common point of concern amongst players and mappers alike: with such a saturated Ranked section, what maps are worth *my* precious time to spend on? It should not come as a surprise that the Ranked section is filled to the brim with beatmaps that come in all styles and genres, and the chances of great maps being missed in the process are very high. As such, the team's goal is to give a second life to recent Ranked beatmaps that may have been overshadowed, by putting their efforts on full display!

This paradigm shift has brought some changes with it, some minor and others less so. First and foremost, the team has seen some drastic changes in preparation for the revival. Firstly, we're reducing scope with fewer members. Secondly, there will be a more active involvement from some NAT members in the hopes of better aligning community expectations on what maps *should* be brought to the spotlight at any given time. Ultimately, we want to make the project officially recognise high-quality beatmapsets, promoting them via the Spotlight beatmap tag. We'll also be forgoing the bi-weekly playlist rotation in favour of a unified month-long seasonal playlist.

What's most important is what's staying, however! The ever-sought-after [Rhythm Incarnate](/wiki/Beatmap_Spotlights#rewards) badge makes its return with a fresh coat of paint, where players can now progressively increase the tier of their RI badge, should they manage to best every other competitor on the seasonal leaderboards. And for the completionists who want to go above and beyond, we'll continue to introduce [pack medals](/wiki/Medals#beatmap-packs) for those users who want to complete and enjoy each season at their own pace.

The first difficulty, [Purple haze](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2458692#mania/5376792), offers a rather straightforward LN release and coordination challenge suitable for anyone who enjoys the approach to this kind of track.
