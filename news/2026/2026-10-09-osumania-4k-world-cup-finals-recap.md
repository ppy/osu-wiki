There may be no [osu! originals](/wiki/Community/Bespoke_music) this time around, but the pool didn't get any less interesting! [Nanahoshi Kangengakudan - MAKE A LOSER (Instrumental Ver.)](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2620235#mania/5880813) (RC8) by ::{ flag=DE }:: [IceDynamix](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8599070) takes the newest and last slot of the rice pool. As the name may suggest, this beatmap has just about everything you'd expect from a wildcard. Making use of dumps, it uses the instrumentals, mainly the sax, as a means for unconventional pattern usage surrounding with the use of said dumps. By doing so, it creates a largely unique experience that allows it stand out far and beyond what a typical slot in this category would. 

We've also been introduced to another newly added hybrid slot in a similar fashion to RC8. This year, however, it has taken a liking to the HB3 slot instead, and [Grizzly Bear - Ready, Able](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2620252#mania/5880849) (HB4) by ::{ flag=PH }:: [doctormango](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/13370527) is... out there, somewhere. If you're familiar with doctormango, you'll already know that the approach taken is going to be extremely free-form. After having seen this in a World Cup environment, we can safely say it has posed quite the accuracy challenge to the players. Doesn't really help that this is below 5 stars, either. Does it really get more deceptive than this?

Ending things off with [Crywolf - Decensus Ad Nihilum](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2620263#mania/5880867) (LN4) by ::{ flag=CN }:: [Hylotl](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/18270260), we're presented with yet another dump chart. Perhaps you could argue that there's an awful lot of these in the pool when compared to previous rounds, but I assure you they all have their own very unique aspects. As expected of the LN category, this consists of very flowy LNs alongside some shield-heavy parts for when the drums really kick in. Overall, every note and every release manages to play very well into each other, resulting in a very solid pick.

The Finals were filled with many veterans from the 4K scene, with China, the United States, and South Korea being amongst the strongest of the bunch. It is precisely why that seeing Australia, Chile, and the United Kingdom making it this far is so exciting to see! By no means should their achievements be overlooked.

In fact, Chile was one of the few teams able to face off against one of The Big Three, going head-to-head with China up until they reached the only tiebreaker of the round. We cannot suggest rewatching the match enough. For those who *didn't* experience it, that's all the more reason to go watch it as soon as possible.

On the other hand, the United Kingdom had a chance to avenge the Chileans after winning over their close Aussie rivals. Unfortunately China wasn't planning on pulling any punches, much less so after their narrow win over Chile, making it to the Grand Finals whereas their soon-to-be competitors, South Korea and the United States, fought over the last spot on the winners' bracket. A battle that would be won by the Koreans, leaving them at an advantage over the United States for the Grand Finals to come the week thereafter.
