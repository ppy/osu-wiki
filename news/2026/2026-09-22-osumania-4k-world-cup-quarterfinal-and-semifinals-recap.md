<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11310625" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Blue_Star_Lotus.jpg')"></a>

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10365024" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Sakura006.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-no-group" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10365024">Sakura006</a></p>

The song tells the story of a young man who repeatedly tries his luck in a casino and loses while trying to win the heart of a girl. How did you interpret the story and the relationship between the two protagonists when approaching your illustrations?

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/15851364" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Creameries2.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-fa" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/15851364">Creameries</a></p>

I actually didn't have any interpretations of the song initially. It was only later during the production that I came up with some additional personalization for the characters. The initial storyline was as simple as it was: the young man walks into the casino and falls in love with a girl. Beyond that, I thought why not add a bit of hesitance on the girl's part?

Our team already had many discussions about the young man winning the heart of the girl, but I failed to really get into the how part. So, with what I call an epiphany, I finally conjured up a new interpretation: initially the girl is uninterested but curious and ready to challenge him. The young man fails multiple times, but each time he fails, he gets back up, still determined to win. It's during these losses that she starts to gain an affinity for him, seeing him as a determined, persevering boy whom she finds endearing. At the end, they both have feelings for each other, and thus they leave the casino with each other by their side.

I think adding that resistance adds so much to the story, in the sense that it no longer looks like a smooth ride. He had to fight for it, and he had his eyes on the prize. That's why I drew the first scene with the girl smiling whilst looking somewhere other than the camera, along with an alternate expression where her mouth is in a frown and her eyes are separated from the eyelashes. I requested that the storyboarder have her frown when she looks at the camera. For the next scene, where they are sitting across from each other, while she isn't frowning, she has more of a light expression. Meanwhile, the young man has a light but stiff expression, with the alternate expression being a frown as he starts to lose. It's a minor thing, really. 

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-09-22-osumania-4k-world-cup-quarterfinal-and-semifinals-recap/1-facial_expressions.png)

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11310625" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Blue_Star_Lotus.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-fa" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11310625">Blue Star Lotus</a></p>

The first time I listened to the song, I imagined the young man trying to win a girl's attention while playing against another guy. After our team exchanged thoughts with the help of the lyrics, we ended up with two characters in the spotlight of the story. With the girl being the young man's opponent, the story became more interesting. As the project went on, our team needed to specify some details of the story so that I, along with the other illustrators, could express the emotions in the remaining illustrations accurately.

Our young man instantly falls for the girl in a casino. Although the girl doesn't know the young man at first, she accepts his invitation to play anyway. As the game goes on, the young man keeps on losing but never backs down, all for her. His determination is what intrigues her.

For the most important part of the story, we needed something that could tell us why she fell for him. Not just because of his determination, but because of the respect, appreciation, sympathy, and love he has for her. This silent message could be delivered with just one thing: a flower.

The game is lost, and the young man is devastated. He knows it's over and decides to give her a red rose. In the end, they "dealt one more time" just for fun, and we see them walking out of the casino together!

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-09-22-osumania-4k-world-cup-quarterfinal-and-semifinals-recap/2-ending_scene.png)

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10365024" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Sakura006.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-no-group" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10365024">Sakura006</a></p>

We spent quite a lot of time during early planning, exploring different ideas for the two protagonists before settling on their final appearances. How did you work together to figure out the personalities and overall vibe that these characters should have? What was the character design process like in the drafting phase?

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/15851364" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Creameries2.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-fa" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/15851364">Creameries</a></p>

Although I wasn't the one who made the character designs, I really didn't want them both to have the typical mafia-inspired look with a red color scheme because that would underutilize other approaches that could've been taken. And since the song is lighthearted and not too serious, I wanted the characters to have a more playful and slightly awkward charm. They still seem competent, but it's clear that they're there to have fun rather than treating the casino like serious business.

One of my personal interpretations is that they're both financially stable and aren't at the casino because they have nothing to gain or lose. They understand that other people may be there for more serious reasons, but that doesn't really affect them. Instead, they're simply there to enjoy themselves, which makes their connection stand out even more in a room filled with people who are scheming, serious, or desperate.

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11310625" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Blue_Star_Lotus.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-fa" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11310625">Blue Star Lotus</a></p>

From the very start, I imagined both the girl and the young man looking fancy to a certain degree and rich enough to gamble responsibly. Main ideas for the design and style were inspired by the Persona game series, jazz, and casinos. The team did mention wanting them to be around 20 to 30 years old, which I completely agreed with. Our third illustrator [bo_yan](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/35145248) was responsible for finalizing the character designs, but I did sketch my versions alongside to speed up the process and exchange ideas. We both agreed that a black, white, red, and gold color palette fit the theme overall, since casinos are known for these colors and they generally represent both wealth and danger. Red also represents romance and love in this case. I was mainly focused on the girl's design. Without a doubt, she had to look gorgeous enough to fall for immediately and to stand out from the crowd. The red-colored dress was the perfect choice for her.

I wasn't the one picking colors for the young man, but I like the choice of white and light blue being present in his design. Light blue being used only on him throughout the entire storyboard hints at him being different from the rest, and it's what makes him stand out as the main character. White and light blue represent purity, innocence, and trustworthiness, which I personally think fit his personality very well.

The lyrics are filled with casino imagery, from cards and dice to chips and roulette. How did you use these elements and the casino setting to build the atmosphere of your illustrations and to keep the visuals connected to the story?

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11310625" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/Blue_Star_Lotus.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-fa" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11310625">Blue Star Lotus</a></p>

We wanted the characters to represent card suits: the heart for the girl and the club for the young man. For that, we sneaked details like the heart-shaped chest window on the girl's dress and the club symbol on the guy's lapel chain into their designs.

Our graphic designers [mohca](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/14499702), [mochasan_](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/23804364), and [Kanomi](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/37694048) also helped to make various assets that would fit the theme, including a full set of card designs. The king, queen, and joker illustrations were completely on me. The king and queen cards feature the same main characters, but with different outfits and accessories. They were inspired by traditional playing card designs, but I wanted to make them look a bit more modern and cool. You can also find more of the symbols I mentioned earlier throughout these artworks. As for the joker, we wanted to avoid giving him a face, or at least any specific identity, since he isn't really the main focus of the story.

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-09-22-osumania-4k-world-cup-quarterfinal-and-semifinals-recap/4&5-card_designs.png)

In the main background art, I wanted to depict the end of the story by drawing the two of them together. She's now the one wearing his white jacket, which I wanted to use to show his caring side by having him keep her warm with his jacket. She's pulling him toward her by his tie, showing her interest in him and maybe even hinting at a kiss. Meanwhile, he's smirking and pulling out a joker card, highlighting his victory in finally winning her heart.

The best way to keep themes consistent is to have many design elements repeat multiple times throughout the entire project. The best way to utilize them is to, well, excuse the pun, "go all in."

There are two specific parts I wish to talk about, the first one being the opening scene with the girl and four other people. One thing that changed during the development of the illustrations was the composition itself. The initial suggestion was to have them both sitting next to each other while playing against a dealer. After further polishing these ideas with [R3aCt10n](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/17020051), one of our storyboarders, we eventually changed it to both of them playing against each other, since I felt that the chemistry between the two characters was still an important part of the story.

The casino setting is still very much present through the cards, chips, heck, even wine glasses and everything else surrounding them, but I wanted the focus to be on the interactions between our protagonists. That's why it was intentional for the other elements and people in the scene to be somewhat dimmed, while our characters remain brighter and more vibrant. In a room filled with all these casino elements and other people playing their own games, the girl essentially becomes the apple of his eye. She is his main interest, and he realizes that with a girl like her, getting her to be interested in him won't be easy. Motivation before the action, maybe?

As for the second part, I wanted to talk about the blackjack game animation. There was a bit of hesitation on my part when [R3aCt10n](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/17020051) suggested actually showing different casino games being played. At first, I wasn't sure how well such a direct approach would fit into the rest of the project, but at the same time, I could see the vision and the point of adding it in. Not only does it add a bit of a "Wow!" factor to the project, but I also think it's quite funny to see these scenes actually being featured. We even spent some time trying out similar games ourselves for additional inspiration while figuring out how these scenes should work.

Having these gameplay moments reinforces the theme instead of having it simply exist in the background. It makes the whole idea feel more committed, and like I said earlier, I like it when certain ideas are approached in different ways. In a way, it's another flavor of "show, don't tell." Instead of simply showing the character already defeated, it's nice to actually see the process of him trying and failing, not once, not twice, but THREE times. [R3aCt10n](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/17020051) implemented these gameplay segments, while [darkos](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/5969271), [kiryuun](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/15231144), and [Mune](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8500334) worked on other storyboard sections throughout the song, each finding different ways to bring the illustrations and recurring casino elements into motion.

The same idea also carries into the intro video, where [Sakura006](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10365024) handled the visual effects design and blended a variety of these elements together before giving our two protagonists their proper debut. I think having all these different parts built around the same visual motifs really helped make the entire production feel connected, even with multiple people working on different sections. Absolute madlads all around, but somehow they simply make it work. Kudos to the whole team!
