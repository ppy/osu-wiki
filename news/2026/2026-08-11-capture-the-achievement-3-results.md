After a fierce 10 days of Capture The Achievement 3, one team has proved their great puzzle hunting and osu! skill, winning by a margin of 38 points!

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-06-07-capture-the-achievement-3/banner.jpg)

In case you're unaware, [Capture The Achievement 3](https://osu.ppy.sh/home/news/2026-06-07-capture-the-achievement-3) is an event that mixes puzzles and competition with various aspects of osu!. Teams contend for points by completing "achievements", which all require a mix of skill, osu! knowledge, general trivia, and puzzle solving.

Now let's check out the top teams and highlight some achievements.

## Top finishers

The top three teams all ended really close together, with there being 38 points between 1st and 2nd, and 59 points between 2nd and 3rd. Throughout the competition though, **🖐️ Pawsome 🖐️** almost never gave up their lead and ended up taking 1st place. For extra context on this team, they placed 2nd in both [CTA1](https://cta.sheppsu.me/iterations/1/teams) and [CTA2](https://osu.ppy.sh/home/news/2025-09-03-capture-the-achievement-2-results).

Congratulations to **🖐️ Pawsome 🖐️** for one-upping their 2nd-place streak, and well played to teams **read COTE** and **data corruption symphony** for making the competition so close, taking 2nd and 3rd place respectively! Below is the players on each team and a breakdown of stats.

<div class="news-chat-quote__text-container">

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11674122" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/willwam.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-no-group" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11674122">willwam</a></p>

"///where.on.earth" was a puzzle achievement that presents you with 3 progressively more zoomed in satellite images of a certain place on earth, and nothing more. Astute online puzzle/arg fans will be able to identify the solve path immediately from just this description and the title - we need to find the location on earth, and then use the site [what3words.com](https://what3words.com/) to extract 3 words for the location, which will give us our target map.

The idea for this one mostly came as "what are some cool ways to hide beatmap instructions?" I knew what3words was going to be fairly constrained in how much you can hide (3 words from a specific set), but I figured I might as well try and add some geolocation to CTA.

The construction for this one was incredibly lucky - I began looking for a list of words I could use, and I also wanted the map to be recognisable enough to account for user error in identifying the exact location - so if a user was slightly off, they could search around the place they found and hopefully stumble across the correct instruction.

I also wanted to make the geolocation portion of the puzzle have some fairly logical footholds even if you aren't a geolocation wizard. The main intended clue was the identifiable airport in the bottom left, but some people also used that the cars were driving on the left in the second image, or just straight up intuition.

Having both pieces come together so elegantly was purely just a stroke of luck and I am personally very happy with how this turned out in the end! Hope you all enjoyed the event, and see you next year!

</div>

<div class="news-chat-quote__text-container">

<a class="avatar news-chat-quote__avatar" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/27780089" style="background-image: url('/wiki/shared/avatars/nnamqahc.jpg')"></a>

<p class="news-chat-quote__username"><a class="news-chat-quote__colour-no-group" href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/27780089">nnamqahc</a></p>

Heavily inspired by the achievements from last year, such as "Outline Guessr", "I Watch osu! Streamers Award", and "Where's Peppy?", I decided to make an achievement that requires a mix of guessing and research. I also wanted to focus on a niche in the game that is often overlooked, as that was one of my favourite parts of the event last year. Originally, this idea centered around skinning in general. However, after doing some research, I worried it would be too difficult to track down original sources due to the sheer volume of skin remixes and the idea was dropped temporarily. The breakthrough came when I was rewatching pishifat's videos (as one does) and stumbled across "osu!mapping: skins". This gave me the idea to base the achievement on custom beatmap skins. It also brought me to the sixth map of the puzzle: Casino Night Zone (2-Player).

The hardest part in creating the achievement is definitely finding the right maps. Not only there is only a handful of maps with custom beatmap skins, there is almost no documentation on them. In addition, I knew from the start that plenty of maps that feature custom skins aren't easily identifiable. To prevent the puzzle from being impossible for the average player, I wanted to ensure each skin was recognizable even without anything else from the map. Fortunately, I managed to use famous maps (aside from Casino Night Zone) to keep it easy.

There's still some parts that I am not completely satisfied with, like having players extract a specific letter from the map, as I don't think it ties in well with the rest of the achievement. However, I promise the achievements will be even better in the next iteration of CTA, so see you next year!

</div>
