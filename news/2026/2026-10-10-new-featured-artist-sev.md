**Sev** exists where the unseen becomes sound (and in our Featured Artist library).

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/572/header.jpg)

As a hobbyist musician for a decade, **Sev** has experimented with countless electronic genres, lately focused on colour bass in the realm of dubstep.

As an *osu! player*, [**SevExists**](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/30544756) also takes into account what kinds of rhythms and soundscapes would be enticing for someone playing along to his tunes.

And as someone who isn't **Sev**, you can grab any of the **8** pre-timed `.osz` files for **Sev**'s music from [his Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/572)! If you're not sure what to do with one of those files, drag it into the game client and [peek at the wiki](/wiki/Client/Beatmap_editor).

Permit your ears to experience some of what **Sev** has to offer:

### Sev - Cosmic Dewdrops

[Mariner](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/1814662) brought **Sev**'s sound to circle clickers through [this map](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2487656), so now your assignment is to play it.
