## EPIC47 Tournament Recap

The last 1v1 tournament at EPIC.LAN featured an absolutely **stacked** bracket stage, with sixteen players of the highest calibre from all eras of osu!’s existence stepping into the elimination stage!

After two days of matches, EPIC43’s winner [rudj](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11592896) defeated the insanely talented [Kingling](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/7010761) **6-2** to reclaim his crown and take home Kettering’s most sought-after rhythm game trophy.

## EPIC49 Tournament

As a new event rolls around, so too does a new tournament! EPIC49 will once again be hosting an **osu!standard 1v1 tournament**, with **Qualifiers** on the **29th October**, and an **Elimination Bracket** on the **30th**, with a *live on stage* **Grand Final** taking place (about) **19:30 BST** on that day! 

With **37** competitors already signed up, an **up to £1500 prize pool**, and a profile badge on the line, now is the perfect time to start warming up for what could be the circle clicking match of your life!

*Find out more about the tournament on its [Forum Page](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/forums/topics/2241806)!*

## Community

Any LAN is about more than just the high-stakes competition, and EPIC.LAN has consistently shown that the community is really what makes events like these special.

From their consistently [incredible performances](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gX431OfHZDs) on the fun tournaments and having some *REAL* contenders for the Community Championship, to even having one team, somehow, **win** the 'world-famous' **EPIC.LAN Pub Quiz**, the osu! community has proven their desire to fulfil the prophecy laid out to them, to truly experience the definition of the motto **“osu! wins everything”**.

**EPIC49** will be no exception, with a wide selection of fun tournaments for people to take part in, the return of the Pub Quiz, stage events, sponsor booths, and even a live comedy show by *award-winning* comedian [John Robertson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Robertson_(comedian))!

*See the full schedule, including all of the fun stuff on the [EPIC.LAN Website](https://www.epiclan.co.uk/epic49/whats-on)!*

## How do I join‽

**EPIC49** will be taking place at the **Kettering Leisure Arena** from the **28th October to 1st November 2026**.
With **72** people *already* seated, it’s the perfect time to consider a trip to what may possibly be the highlight of your year, and a chance to meet up with people (and have a few drinks – but *please drink responsibly*) from this incredible shared experience we all call osu!

To find out more about this upcoming event, check out the tournament’s [forum thread](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/forums/topics/2241806), and join the [osu!UK discord](https://discord.gg/yB2ZFap) for updates on EPIC49 and any future events!
