Welcome to the recap of the osu!mania Mapping World Cup 2026!

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-08-22-osumania-mapping-world-cup-2026-concludes/MMWC26.jpg)

The osu!mania Mapping World Cup is a country-based, multi-stage osu!mania mapping contest on a scale unlike anything seen previously in the osu!mania scene. After competing across multiple rounds of mapping, one country emerged as this year’s winner.

For more information on the rules, contest format, schedule, submissions, and judging criteria, please refer to the contest’s [wiki page](/wiki/Contests/MMWC/2026) and [forum post](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/forums/topics/2185948).

## Results

![MMWC 2026 Podium](/wiki/shared/news/2026-08-22-osumania-mapping-world-cup-2026-concludes/MMWC26_Podium.jpg)

After three stages of the mapping phase, featuring a total of 72 charted maps, ::{ flag=ID }:: Team Indonesia has successfully claimed the crown on August 15, surpassing the contenders from the American continent: second-place ::{ flag=US }:: ::{ flag=CA }:: Team American Alliance and third-place ::{ flag=US }:: Team United States. A list of the participating teams, charted beatmaps, stage activity, and complete breakdown of the contest results plus links to judge's comment can be found in the [contest spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qP-zPogcytTcMB3SMIAQkWYiTcxOzfMXo4gauRmORbI/edit?usp=sharing).

## Prizes

The top three teams have earned their well-deserved rewards, with a total prize pool of $250 USD, alongside a few additional goodies:

- **1st place:** Cash prize equal to 55% of the total prize pool, [contest points](/wiki/Contests/Contest_points), and a shiny profile badge.
- **2nd place:** Cash prize equal to 30% of the total prize pool and [contest points](/wiki/Contests/Contest_points).
- **3rd place:** Cash prize equal to 15% of the total prize pool and [contest points](/wiki/Contests/Contest_points).
