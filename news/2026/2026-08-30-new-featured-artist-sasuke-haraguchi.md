Anyone who appreciates Vocaloid already knows **Sasuke Haraguchi**, our latest Featured Artist!

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/561/header.jpg)

Born in 2003, **Sasuke Haraguchi** is a Tokyo-based musician who's been surrounded by music since the beginning. He began composing at the age of five and made his major-label debut at fifteen, followed by continuous progress through collaborations with other high profile artists (like *m-flo* and *Soushi Sakiyama*) and spots in major commercials (like one with *Apple*!).

**Sasuke Haraguchi**'s Vocaloid tracks *HITO Mania* and *Igaku* topped Vocaloid-specific music charts, and unsurprisingly found their way to osu! shortly afterwards. 

While things like achieving *Japan's 30 Under 30* and playing live shows across the country are impressive, we think **Sasuke Haraguchi** joining osu!'s Featured Artists is pretty cool too.

**6** songs are part of [**Sasuke Haraguchi**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/561). Keep in mind that every minute you spend contemplating whether or not to download one of these pre-timed `.osz` files is a minute you could be spending in the editor.
