This isn't your average everyday darkness. This is **kuro**.

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/369/header.jpg)

Renowned across the rhythm game scene, **kuro** has been producing tunes fully spanning the electronic spectrum for nearly a decade. Featured in games like *SOUND VOLTEX*, *Polaris Chord*, *Lanota* and *Rotaeno*, **kuro**'s sound is likely familiar to anyone who frequents their local arcade (or anyone who plays rhythm games on their phone).

[**kuro**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/369) is now home to **7** new tracks. Are they available as pre-timed `.osz` files? Probably. You can confirm for yourself though.

Today may be Halloween, but we're not going to deliver jumpscares. Here are some previews of **kuro**'s tracks instead:
