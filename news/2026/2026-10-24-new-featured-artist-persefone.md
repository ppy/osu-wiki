While most complex rhythm game music is made by electronic producers, death metal band **Persefone** does things the old-fashioned way.

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/574/header.jpg)

Over the course of 20+ years, **Persefone** has proven themselves to be a progressive powerhouse. They've released 6 studio albums and played more live shows than we can reasonably count. Everything the band does proves just how impressive their compositions and technical performances are, and now that prowess is part of osu!'s Featured Artists.

Migrate over to [**Persefone**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/574) for pre-timed `.osz` files of **10** tracks from the band's album *Spiritual Migration*.

If you need some convincing before that, look no further than these previews:

### Persefone - Consciousness

This two-part track has been mapped in full by [GoldenWolf](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/1612624), so [experience it for yourself](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/700870) or watch this ancient video of [wuk](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/14457718) giving it an attempt from [COE](/wiki/Community/cavoe's_osu!_event)! (Alternatively, try out [Fabrical](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/10719991)'s [4K osu!mania rendition](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2361305) of the song.)
