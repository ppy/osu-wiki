Hey, you've finally **awoKen**.

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/554/header.jpg)

American producer **awoKen** has been producing jazzy electro tunes since the early 2020s. Inspired by the likes of [*BilliumMoto*](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/38), *mommy3125*, *kosu.*, and the breadth of video game soundtracks existing in the aether, this tune crafter knows how to produce soundscapes equally familiar and foreign to rhythm games.

[**awoKen**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/554) has **5** tracks for you to peruse. Grab a pre-timed `.osz` when your mapping muscles are warmed up.

If your mapping muscles need some greasing first, these previews might do the trick:
