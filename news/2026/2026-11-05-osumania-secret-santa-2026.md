The holidays are approaching, and that means another osu!mania Secret Santa Contest!

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-11-05-osumania-secret-santa-2026/banner.jpg)

The **osu!mania Secret Santa Contest 2026** is an osu!mania mapping contest where mappers create beatmaps based on a randomly received short text prompt from another mapper. And this year, we're opening it up so that mappers who missed the prompt submission deadline can still participate in the mapping contest via *judge-written prompts*!

You can discuss this event in the [official discussion thread](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/forums/topics/2259566) or the public [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/yJkGNqascN).

The limit is your imagination!

**Judge-written prompts** will be posted through the discussion thread and Discord server as soon as the mapping phase begins.

Results will be made public through the Discord server as well as through a conclusion news post, alongside a live showcase of the results, top 8 submissions and more on the [osuBSM Twitch channel](https://www.twitch.tv/osubsm), with the schedule to be announced through the discussion thread and Discord server.

## Prizes

Top 8 submissions will receive 1 month each of [osu!supporter](/wiki/osu!supporter)! The winning submission will also receive a profile badge (pending).

And all participants receive the most wondrous prize of all: the gift of giving!

## Judging criteria

| Category | Details |
| :-: | :-- |
| Prompt (10) | How well the beatmap responds to the given text prompt. |
| Creativity (10) | How artfully crafted and expressed the beatmap is. |
| Gameplay (10) | How engaging the beatmap is, and how enhancing it is to the experience of playing along to the music. |
| Musical Representation (10) | How properly the beatmap follows the music's macro- and micro-level structures (i.e. form, repetitions), sounds and/or sound contours (applicable for dumps), etc. |

Each judge gives a score out of 40, and a submission's final score will be the standardised sum of all the judges' scores.

## Rules

Please submit contest materials on time. We would prefer not to have to chase people down for these things.

### Prompt

- Prompt **must** adhere to [osu! rules](/wiki/Rules) where applicable.
- Prompt **must not** exceed 5 words in length and may only contain alphanumeric characters or common punctuations. Hyphenated words count as multiple words.
- Prompt **must not** be a song or artist name or in direct reference to one. Indirect references are okay, but use them at your own discretion.
- Prompt may be a descriptive genre/style, or abstract concept/phrase. Please feel free to exercise creative liberties here.
- We will screen for prompts that we deem too easy or too difficult, and notify you if we believe your prompt requires changes.

Your prompt along with your osu! username will be passed along to another mapper, chosen at random, who has also submitted a prompt. You must create a beatmap based on the prompt you receive.

The organisers reserve the right to disqualify or request revisions for any submission, but will ensure that the prompt submitter will be informed in a timely manner. Only those with submitted prompts may receive another mapper's prompt, so an outright disqualification for a prompt submission, which includes missing the final prompt revision deadline, will result in having to map using one of our **judge-written prompts**.

### Beatmap submission

- Submission must be in `.osz` format.
- Submission should be an osu!mania beatmap in a key mode that abides by the [ranking criteria](/wiki/Ranking_criteria/osu!mania).
- The length of the beatmap must be **between 1:25 and 7:30**.
- Submission must only contain **one difficulty**.
- Submission should have a **background of reasonable quality**.
- Hitsounds and storyboards are optional. They may be taken into account in judging.
- The difficulty name should be **identical to the prompt received**.
- Submissions must not be publicly revealed until results are out, which includes sharing the map and/or submitting it through the editor.
- Your submission must be solely made by you. **Collaborations are not allowed** for this contest!
- **Any use of generative artificial intelligence (gAI)**, whether it be for the background, song, beatmap, or any other asset present among your submitted files, will be subject to review, and will likely result in immediate disqualification. For edge cases (i.e. using AI to anonymise your voice, which has been done in previous Secret Santa contests), please ask ::{ flag=US }:: [-mint-](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/8976576) or ::{ flag=US }:: [elexire](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/9206093) first and make sure you have our approval before proceeding.

Note that all submissions will be anonymised before they're reviewed by judges to avoid bias. During this process, storyboards and backgrounds may be removed.

## Prompt and beatmap submission

To receive a prompt from another user for your beatmap, you must submit a prompt via the **[prompt submission form](https://form-auth.ppy.sh/form/2026-mania-secret-santa-contest-prompt-submission)** by the draft deadline (November 10th). Any revisions must also be submitted via this form by the revision deadline (November 12th).

You may submit your beatmap through the **[contest page](https://mappersguild.com/contests/listing?contest=6ae9370a234e251a6af18929)**. You are allowed to resubmit your entry anytime before the mapping phase has ended.

- When you've finished mapping, create an `.osz` file through the editor via `File > Export Package` in osu!(stable), or `File > Export > For compatibility (.osz)` in osu!(lazer).
- Once exported, upload the `.osz` file to a file sharing service, making sure the link is accessible to anyone, and paste the link into the submission box in the [contest page](https://mappersguild.com/contests/listing?contest=6ae9370a234e251a6af18929).
