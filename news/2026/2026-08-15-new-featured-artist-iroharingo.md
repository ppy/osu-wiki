If the only colours you can see are black/white/red, you're probably living in **irohaRingo**'s universe.

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/557/header.jpg)

Vocaloid producer **irohaRingo** has been releasing dark and provocative pop-rock music since 2018. While all of his music is widely appreciated by the Vocaloid community, his track "Why I hate you" managed to reach beyond the bubble with millions of listens across various platforms.

One platform that has especially appreciated that song is osu!, where [Ryuusei Aika](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/7777875)'s [map of "Why I hate you"](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets/2263303) has amassed nearly a million plays. 90% of those plays are retries within the first 15 seconds, but let's skip over that for now. **irohaRingo** makes songs that osu! players love, so he's now part of our Featured Artist library!

[**irohaRingo**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/557) is home to **10** songs (in the form of pre-timed beatmap files), so there's nothing stopping you from opening the editor and composing circles to your heart's content.
