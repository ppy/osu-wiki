What started as a humble Indonesian osu!catch tournament has grown all across Asia across the last few years. Now, it's ready for the world.

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-09-16-catch-indonesia-cup-open-2026-registrations-now-open/banner.jpg)

## What is it?

[Catch Indonesia Cup Open 2026](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/forums/topics/2241677), or CICO 2026 for short, is an international 1v1 open-rank osu!catch tournament that aims to celebrate the osu!catch community as a whole. As the name suggests, the tournament kicked off as a [grassroots competition for Indonesian players](/wiki/Tournaments/CIC/2012) before evolving into a continental tournament and eventually becoming what is effectively one of — if not the — biggest 1v1 tournament in the osu!catch scene right now.

## What's new?

Aside from the fresh "sweets" theme, for its 14th iteration, CICO will be expanded into the global stage for the very first time. Yes, that means everyone from everywhere in the world is free to join now!

And just like last year, CICO 2026 will also be packed and loaded with **10+** brand-new custom songs along with plenty more of custom beatmaps to boot! For a sampler of what's to come, take a look at the following playlist of all the custom songs from the previous tournament:

https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL7WjLkQAh7tFDo605CsVHnttzAzYX4lNS I FORGOT HOW TO EMBED YOUTUBE PLAYLIST HELP ;~;

Finally, following the footsteps of this year's [osu!catch World Cup](/wiki/Tournaments/CWC/2026), CICO 2026 will not feature any convert beatmaps for the first time (i.e. all the beatmaps in the mappool will be osu!catch-specific).

## Tournament schedule

| Event | Dates |
| --: | :-- |
| Registration phase | 17 September – 30 September (23:59 UTC) |
| Qualifiers | 23 October – 25 October |
| Round of 32 | 30 October – 1 November |
| Round of 16 | 6 November – 8 November |
| Quarterfinals | 13 November – 15 November |
| Semifinals | 20 November – 22 November |
| Finals | 27 November – 29 November |
| Grand Finals | 4 December – 6 December |

Matches will be streamed on the [@osucatchID](https://www.twitch.tv/osucatchid) Twitch channel, with [@customallosu](https://www.twitch.tv/customallosu) serving as a secondary channel for matches with conflicting schedules. All archived VODs will also be kept on the [osu!catch Indonesia Youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@osucatchID) for viewing after the matches.

## How do I register?

[![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-09-16-catch-indonesia-cup-open-2026-registrations-now-open/register-here.png)](https://wybin.xyz/cico2026)

The deadline to register for this tournament is 30 September 2026 (23:59 UTC).

To be eligible to participate, you must:

- Be a member of the [CICO 2026 Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/SdT9dvW).
- Not have violated the [osu! community rules](/wiki/Rules) within the past 12 months.

For more information, don't hesitate to [check out the tournament's wiki](/wiki/Tournaments/CIC/Open_2026) or contact one of the hosts directly. We hope to see you around!
