# Note (disambiguation)

**Note** can have multiple meanings:

## Gameplay

- An [osu!mania note](/wiki/Gameplay/Hit_object/Note), the hit object played with a single keypress
- A [Don or a Kat](/wiki/Gameplay/Hit_object/Hit), the circular hit objects of osu!taiko
- A [hit circle](/wiki/Gameplay/Hit_object/Hit_circle), the osu! hit object tapped at a single point in time

## Music

- A single sound in a song, the length of which is measured relative to a [beat](/wiki/Music_theory/Beat)
