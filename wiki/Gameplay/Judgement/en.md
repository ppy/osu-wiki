# Judgement

Judgment is the system that determines what timing result you get when you hit an object.
-300 gives you full accuracy on a note
-100 gives you 33.33% of the accuracy value 
-50 gives you 16.67% of the accuracy value
-Miss gives you 0%, meaning you missed the notes timing entirely.
These numbers will contribute to the accuracy you have no the map overall.
