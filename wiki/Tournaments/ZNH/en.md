# Zenith LAN

![Zenith LAN logo](img/banner.png)

The **Zenith LAN** (***ZNH***) is a single-elimination 1v1 osu! tournament hosted by ::{ flag=GR }:: [KakashiSensei_](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/18254930) in Thessaloniki, Greece as part of [Animecon: Run Thessaloniki VIII](https://anime-con.gr/)'s community program. It is the first major LAN osu! tournament to ever be held in the city of Thessaloniki. Come and have fun in an Anime Convention with a lot of things to do including seeing osu! players, space to play osu! freely and join us on Saturday 3rd of October where we have an Osu! Panel talking about the game.

## Ruleset

### Basic rules

1. Zenith LAN 2026 is a open rank LAN event with an online qualifier stage.
2. The tournament will be played in osu!lazer.
3. There are no rank requirements for the tournament.
4. The LAN event will be held as a single elimination tournament starting from the Round of 16.
5. All registered participants will be submitted for official osu! account support screening.
6. Participants may take part in qualifiers before screening is completed. If a participant is removed following screening, their qualifier result will be treated as void and they will not be permitted to participate in the LAN bracket.
7. Players are not allowed to self-referee their own matches.
8. A dedicated referee will be assigned to each match and another referee will be provided within 2–5 minutes if necessary.
9. Players have 5 minutes from the scheduled match time to join the lobby.
10. Cheating, match manipulation, rule violations, and other serious issues may be investigated and reported to the appropriate osu! staff.
11. Tournament staff may perform reasonable checks of tournament equipment when necessary to investigate suspected cheating.
12. Players may use their own peripherals as permitted by tournament staff.
13. Match results and tournament records will be made publicly available on the main sheet.
14. Every participant must be part of the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/Y4SF29mhHQ).

### Qualifiers

1. The qualifiers will be played in a playlist.
2. The maps will be played in the order they appear on the sheet.
3. Once the qualifiers are over, the 16 highest-seeded players will advance to the LAN bracket.
4. Once the qualifier period ends, the playlist will close and no further scores will be accepted.
5. Players will have 18 attempts for 13 maps that they can distribute across the playlist, with their best score on each beatmap being used for the final qualifier results.
6. Participants will be required to submit a score on every map in order to be eligible for seeding.
7. The qualifiers will allow all participants to complete the qualifier mappool individually within a predefined time window.
8. For seeding, average score will be used and standard seeding will apply.

### LAN bracket

1. The two players will `/roll`. The winner decides whether to choose the order of picks or bans, and the loser chooses the order of the remaining selection.
2. The win condition for the matches, as well the as number of bans available to each player, depends on the round as follows:
   - Round of 16: Best of 5 (first to 3), with 2 bans per player.
   - Quarterfinals and Semifinals: Best of 9 (first to 5), with 2 bans per player.
   - Finals and Grand Finals: Best of 15 (first to 7), with 1 ban per player.
3. During their turn, players will be given a maximum of 2 minutes to pick a map and an additional 1.5 minutes after the pick to be ready.
4. Time between the pick and the start of the map: 1:30 minutes.
